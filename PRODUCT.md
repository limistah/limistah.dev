# Product: limistah.dev

## Register
brand

## Users
Engineers, engineering managers, and tech leads evaluating Aleem Isiaka as a backend/cloud architect — either for collaboration, hiring, or learning. Also fellow developers who follow his writing.

## Brand
Terminal-native, authoritative. The site should feel like reading a well-structured system design document: dense with meaning, no decoration for its own sake. The person browsing at 11pm deciding whether to reach out expects technical credibility at a glance.

## Tone
Direct. No fluff. Sentences earn their place. Technical precision without jargon dumping. Writing reads like someone who has actually built the things they describe.

## Anti-references
- SaaS marketing pages with gradient text and hero metrics
- "Creative developer" portfolios with cursor trails and full-page takeovers
- Blogger-template sites with excessive whitespace and oversized headings
- Generic dark-theme developer portfolios that look like every Tailwind starter kit

## Strategic principles
- The writing is the product. The design frames it, never competes.
- Dark-only. No toggle. A backend architect's site should feel like a terminal.
- Monospace where it signals precision (dates, tags, labels, logo). Serif for long prose. Sans for navigation and UI chrome.
- Green (primary), Cyan (links/interactive), Amber (highlights) — three accents, used deliberately.
