I take model output as an initial draft, then revise and review until it meets my requirements and coding style. When something looks off, I query the model for its reasoning — a kind of two-factor verification against slop. I also read my own PRs properly before asking anyone else to.

I care more about my reputation as an engineer than about brain rot. Once a name is tagged to a piece of work, the means stops justifying the end. Responsibility has never been more important.

Hermes — my private tutor

I run Hermes locally, for now. The job is narrow and personal: research for me, teach me French, sharpen my English, keep my Arabic from going rusty. Paired with ddts for voice transcription, so pronunciation drilling never leaves the terminal.

Where this is heading

Using hosted models for everyday inference is a risk worth naming — it's surprising how much these systems already know about us. Where latency allows, self-hosting open-weight models makes sense. On-device is likely the better long-run route, and a few projects are already there.
