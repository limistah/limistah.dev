As a boy back in the hood, I was regarded as a weakling, I did not withstand pain as much as my peers could. During break periods while attending primary school, I would play as the back-man(defensive position) in a [monkey post](https://seye.medium.com/monkey-post-68e641c01e97) football game, and of I was excellent at it. My peers would go on to do different _extreme_ but perfected acrobatic stunts - dingba, back-flips, high-jumps, but I would rather remain a spectator, in complete awe of these precious abilities.

I can also recall my parents waking us up as early as 5am to get water for the entire household. That single trick would help us have a terrific early mornings that helped us arrive early at school before the 8am [morning assemblies](https://share.google/aimode/ISD7S5GybwL9iu4qg) conducted in Nigerian schools.

This month, I completed one of my life goals that would be very impossible due to my circumstances. So, I wonder: was it a bit of luck, charm or just pure habit?

Honestly, I can't tell. In my perspective, I was only doing things that I needed to do even if they seemed irrational and very unrewarding to my friends. And in retrospect, I am asking how were they possible, and after much reflection, I found the answer: old habits, die hardest!
