As a young boy back then in the hood, I am known as one of the weaklings, I did not withstand pain as much as my peers could. I remember, during break periods during at my primary school, I would play the backman in a [monkey post](https://seye.medium.com/monkey-post-68e641c01e97) football game, and of course I was very good at it. My peers would go on to do different _extreme_ but perfected acrobatic acrobatic stunts- dingba, back-flips, high-jumps, but I would rather remain a spectator, in complete awe of these precious abilities.

Still, as a child, my parents would wake us as early as 5am in the morning to get water for the entire household, this single trick would help us have the most robust early mornings and still arrive at school before 8am that [morning assemblies](https://share.google/aimode/ISD7S5GybwL9iu4qg) are conducted in Nigerian schools. Also, every Monday-Wednesday, we make a minimum of 2kilometers walk to and from my Dad's Madrasah, and also repeat this morning to afternoon on weekends and any public holidays that falls on the madrasah days. And these will not do justice to my childhood.

This month, I completed a set of educational feat that would be very impossible for someone as busy as myself. Was it a bit of luck, charm or just pure habit? I can't tell, for me I was just doing things that I needed to do even if they seemed irrational and very unrewarding to my friends, looking back I am asking how was I able to achieve them, after much reflection, I found it, old habits, die hardest!
