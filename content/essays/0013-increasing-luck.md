title: Hard work increases luck
date: 2026-10-10
summary: I have worked harder without realizing that I was, consecuently, great things have happened in my life counting me amongst the lucky ones. Even right now that I tend to have slowed down but subconciously work harder, I now ask could this be luck or what is replicable?
---

As a young boy back then in the hood, I am known as one of the weaklings, I did not withstand pain as much as my peers could. I remember, during break periods during the primary school days, I would play the backman in a monkey post football game, and of course I was very good at it. My peers would go on to do different _extreme_ but perfected acrobatic stunts- dingba, back flips, high jumps, but I would rather end up as a spectator, in complete jealous of this precious abilities.

Still, as a child, my parents would wake us as early as 5am in the morning to get water for the entire household, this single trick would help us have the most robust early mornings and still arrive at school before 8am that assemblies are conducted in Nigeria. Also, every Monday-Wednesday, we will walk minimum of 2kilometers to and from my Dad's Madrasah, also ensuring we have the most packed days of any of our peers.

In hindsight, these were not torture but the recipe for what would eventually help me navigate teenage and adulthood.
